How to Evaluate a Bookkeeping Partner

Accurate Transaction Volume Assessment

Ensure your package provides enough coverage without paying for unused capacity. Your needs differ at 50 vs. 500 transactions per month.

The "Gold Standard" of Monthly Reconciliations

Every bank account and credit card must be balanced against your ledger every thirty days. This is non-negotiable for accuracy.

QuickBooks Specialization

Your partner must be an expert in the tools you use to ensure seamless data integration and prevent costly software-related errors.

Active Mentorship & Communication

Look for a strategic partner who helps you interpret data and highlight trends, not just a data entry clerk.
